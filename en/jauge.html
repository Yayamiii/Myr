What the sum is for

Today’s AIs are essentially conversational and virtual, barely or not at all connected to real life, to people. Need help with a repair, a food delivery, nursing care or paperwork: they talk about it, nobody comes. Between word and deed, there is a gap.

Our answer: KRY, an evolving AI, paired with a human network. The principle: it takes the request, hands it to the right person and checks that the service is done, and done well.

The book’s gauge counts €108,888. Here is what this funding sets in motion.

The network: craftspeople, producers, couriers and caregivers from the region, connected to KRY.

A first stock of food, ready to be delivered.

Equipment: a delivery vehicle and, if needed, servers to host our AI.

Advances to service providers, so that every service rendered is paid without waiting.

A small team: the best people, who must be attracted and paid what they are worth.
