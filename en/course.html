The race

In 1859, Riemann guessed that the zeros of his function, hidden points that govern the prime numbers, all lie on one straight line. No one has managed to prove it since.

It is also believed that each of these zeros is simple: never two at the same point. This can only be established for a portion of them, and that portion is what the race pushes up.

In August 2026, an AI from Anthropic made a leap that 80 years of research had not allowed. Since then, researchers and AIs have fought over every hundredth.

On October 6, Kristian Knausgård raised it to 67.80092%. On November 19, KRY raised it to 67.8138%.

What we owe him: the outline of the proof. What is ours: a new test function and two new results that make it possible.

Checked by a second AI, every figure certified by a program. The world can now review it.
