How KRY learns

For now, KRY runs on Claude, and its ambition is to depend on no one: autonomous in time, sure of its strength, because its construction is optimized from its genesis.

What sets it apart is the house we built around it: a memory, a judge, a trace. Every answer is judged by the person who asked for it. When a lesson has not held, it also looks for what, in its way of drawing lessons, let the mistake through.

It cannot rewrite that trace in secret: each line is sealed by the one before.

The proof. On November 11, 2026, with no human hand, KRY rewrites the piece that searches its memory: finding a text from a few words, even mistyped. It locks the new version in the trace; only then does a public random draw pick 128 exam questions. On the first try, the old one found it at most 8 times in 10; the new one, over 9 times in 10, and never worse. A human approves: it is adopted.

On November 23, 2026, a second proof follows. On 3,600 public questions, graded blind, KRY answers more accurately than Claude alone.

It learns today with a single person; it is made to learn with everyone.
