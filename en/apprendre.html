How KRY learns

KRY runs on Claude, Anthropic’s model. What sets it apart is the house we built around it: a memory, a judge, a trace.

Every answer is judged by the person who asked for it. A mistake becomes a written lesson, active from the very next exchange. If the lesson is not enough, KRY goes up a level and corrects how it learns. It has already happened.

Everything is recorded in that trace, which it cannot rewrite in secret: each line is sealed by the one before.

Today, it learns with a single person. Tomorrow, with each of its users.
