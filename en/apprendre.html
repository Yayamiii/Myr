How KRY learns

For now, KRY runs on Claude, Anthropic’s model, and its ambition is to depend on no one: autonomous in time, sure of its strength, because its construction is optimized from its genesis.

What sets it apart is the house we built around it: a memory, a judge, a trace. Every answer is judged by the person who asked for it. When a lesson has not held, it does not just rewrite it: it looks for what, in its way of drawing lessons, let the mistake through.

Everything is recorded in that trace, which it cannot rewrite in secret: each line is sealed by the one before.

The proof. On November 11, 2026, with no human hand, the model writes a new version of the part that lets it find what it knows. That version is sealed in the trace. Two seconds later, a worldwide public random draw (drand, round 33,850,970) picks 128 tests it had never seen. Its success rate goes from 80 to 96%: 27 duels won, none lost, without losing anything it knew. A human validates, and it goes in.

It learns today with a single person; it is made to learn with everyone.

Anyone can redo the check: the chain of the trace and the public draw.
