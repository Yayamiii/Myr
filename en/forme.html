Everything has a root.

The shared gesture

Goldbach: every even number greater than 2 is the sum of two primes. Checked up to four billion billion, never proved.

Riemann: the zeros of his function all lie on one straight line. Proved in November 2026.

Hodge: certain objects in space are all built from simple pieces, made of equations.

All three obey the same symmetry: a movement, its reflection, and the exact point where the two meet. For Goldbach, it is the middle of the number. For Riemann, a line. For Hodge, a diagonal.

This symmetry shows where to look. It inspires the way KRY builds itself: review what it has done, correct, begin again.
