Everything has a root.

One shape

Goldbach, 1742: every even number greater than 2 is the sum of two primes. Checked up to four billion billion, never proved.

Riemann, 1859: the zeros of his function all lie on one straight line. One million dollars for whoever settles it.

Hodge, 1950: certain objects in space are all built from simple pieces, made of equations. Same prize.

Placed side by side, they make the same gesture: a movement, its reflection, and the exact point where the two meet. For Goldbach, it is the middle of the number. For Riemann, a line. For Hodge, a diagonal.

This figure shows where to look. It inspires the way KRY builds itself: review what it has done, correct, begin again.
