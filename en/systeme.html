The system

It starts from the WHO’s health map: 50,670 concepts, from diseases to care, arranged like a tree. Each resource is placed where it speaks, and its effect spreads as logic requires: half stays in place, a quarter rises to broader concepts, a quarter descends to finer ones. So each one reaches the whole corpus, in its fair measure.

On the island, that means 1,033 plants joined by 6,382 links, drawn from 41 sources, from 1862 to 2025; every link keeps its quotation and its page.

People ask it questions in their own words. It finds where the question falls on the map, and what the writings say about it. When they fall silent, so does it.

It is a holistic vision of health, built for the good of each person, not for money. For another country, only the library changes.
