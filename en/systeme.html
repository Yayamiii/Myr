Everything has a root.

The system

It links three worlds that did not speak to each other: the WHO’s great medical map and its 50,670 entries, traditional knowledge, scientific studies.

1,033 plants of Réunion Island are attached to it by 6,382 links. Each one carries its quotation and its page: 41 sources, from 1862 to 2025.

People ask it questions in their own words. It finds where the question falls on that map, and what the writings say about it. When they fall silent, so does it.

Built in one month. For another country, the method stays the same: only the library changes.

Next step: medicines, explained simply, to talk about them better with a doctor.

Documented traditional uses. Never a substitute for medical advice.
