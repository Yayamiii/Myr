The system

It starts from the WHO’s health map and its 50,670 entries, and links to it all the human resources built around it: testimonies, books, ancestral knowledge, scientific studies. Each one counts.

1,033 plants of Réunion Island are attached to it by 6,382 links, each with its quotation and its page: 41 sources, from 1862 to 2025.

People ask it questions in their own words. It finds where the question falls on the map, and what the writings say about it. When they fall silent, so does it.

It is a holistic vision of health, built for the good of each person, not for money. For another country, the method stays the same: only the library changes.
